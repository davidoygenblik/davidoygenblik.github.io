Excited to be presenting my work, *VillainNet: Targeted Poisoning Attacks Against SuperNets Along the Accuracy-Latency Pareto Frontier*
at the 32nd ACM Conference on Computer and Communications Security! :)

Unfortunately, page limit restrictions by ACM prevent officially released papers from having more than 15 pages of content (including appendices and references). This means that the official paper is missing key appendix information that answers important questions about the VNET methodology. Consequently, I have uploaded the complete paper (with all appendix sections) on my website which can be found with the following link:  

[VillainNet: Targeted Poisoning Attacks Against SuperNets Along the Accuracy-Latency Pareto Frontier](https://davidoygenblik.github.io/pdfs/VNET.pdf)

The official shortened version will be linked to here as soon as it is uploaded by ACM.

Click below to watch the presentation:

[![](https://img.youtube.com/vi/H1fyPD8vWDo/maxresdefault.jpg)](https://youtu.be/H1fyPD8vWDo?si=KL8klwj6Uc7dUTcY)

VillainNet received all three badges (Available, Functional, and Results Reproducible) from the artifact evaluation committee. The artifact can be found [here.](https://zenodo.org/records/17073435)

A big thanks to the CyFI lab and GT SAIL for contributions to the project/paper, ultimately leading to a wonderful trip to Taiwan!
